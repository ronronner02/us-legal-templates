Before Filing a Complaint 
Before you file a Pharmacy Benefit Management Company (PBM) complaint with the 
Vermont Department of Financial Regulation (DFR), you should first contact the PBM to 
resolve the issue. Document phone calls to the company, keep copies of all written 
communications, and complete all levels of contractual appeals available. If you are still 
unable to resolve your complaint, you may contact DFR for assistance. Refer to Section III of 
the complaint form for a list of laws and regulations applying to PBMs for which DFR can 
provide assistance. 
Instructions for Filing a PBM Complaint 
To file a complaint against a PBM for violation of law or rule, (1) fill out this PBM Complaint 
Form to the best of your ability and (2) submit along with any supporting documentation 
via email at:  dfr.insuranceinfo@vermont.gov
