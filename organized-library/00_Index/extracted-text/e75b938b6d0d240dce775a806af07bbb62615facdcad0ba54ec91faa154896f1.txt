INSTRUCTIONS AND FORM FOR NOTICE OF HEARING 
Please note that court staff cannot provide you with legal advice or assist you in 
completing these forms.   The use of these forms does not guarantee you will be 
successful in court.   It is highly encouraged that you speak with an attorney if you are 
needing assistance.   If you have specific questions regarding the forms, you may 
contact the legal form helpline at 1 -855-784-0004 or email UJS staff at 
ujssrlhelp@ujs.state.sd.us.   Additionally, for more information on South Dakota laws, you 
can also visit the South Dakota Legislature website. 
IMPORTANT NOTICE 
If a hearing is set on your Motion, the Notice of Hearing must be mailed to the opposing 
party at least twenty days before the hearing is held. 
Complete this form by: 
1. Complete the caption by filling in the county name, judicial circuit number, 
Plaintiff and Defendant name, and file number. All of this can be found on any 
previous filing completed in your case. Leave the remainder of the form blank 
for the Court to fill out. 
a. You can also contact the Clerk of Court, legal help line, or email UJS staff 
for this information. 
2. File the original copies of the Notice of Hearing (UJS -336), Motion (UJS -335), 
Affidavit in Support (UJS -337), if you completed one, and any supporting 
documents with the Clerk of Court. Remember to retain copies for your records. 
3. The Court will provide you with a completed Notice of Hearing (UJS-336) which 
will set the date, time, and location of the hearing. 
4. Mail copies of the Notice of Hearing (UJS-336), Motion and Request for Hearing 
(UJS-335), and Affidavit in Support (UJS -337), if you complete one, and any 
supporting documents to the opposing party at least twenty days before the 
hearing. 
5. Complete an Affidavit of Mailing (UJS-338) and file with the Clerk of Court.
