TO THE ABOVE-NAMED DEFENDANT(S):
1. You or your attorney must file a written defense (usually called an "Answer") within 20 days following the 
date you received this summons. The 20 days starts to run the day after you were served. This Answer must be

Answer to the attorney instead of the Plaintiff. The name and address of each Plaintiff or Plaintiff's attorney are listed 
in the attached lawsuit. You must file a certificate of service, which may be filed with your Answer, stating the date you 
served your Answer on each Plaintiff or Plaintiff's attorney and the method you used to serve it.
