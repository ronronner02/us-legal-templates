MINIMUM WAGE
 ▶ Every worker must be paid at least minimum wage. Few exceptions apply.
 ▶ The minimum wage goes up every year. These rates are in effect from July 1, 2026 to June 
30, 2027. The next minimum wage increase is on July 1, 2027.
 ▶ Using tips to cover minimum wage is illegal in Oregon.
 ▶ Deductions from minimum wage are allowed if legally required (like taxes) or if you agree 
in writing and the deduction is for your private benefit. Your paycheck must show this infor-
mation. Deductions from minimum wage for uniforms, tools, or employer-required lodging 
are prohibited.
 ▶ If you make close to minimum wage, you may qualify for the Earned Income Tax Credit.
