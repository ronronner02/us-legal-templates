10.12. Methamphetamine Disclosure.  If Seller knows that methamphetamine was ever manufactured, processed, 117 
cooked, disposed of, used or stored at the Home or Lot, Seller is required to disclose such fact. No disclosure is required if the Home 118 
and Lot were remediated in accordance with state standards and other requirements are fulfilled pursuant to § 25-18.5-102, C.R.S., 119 
Buyer further acknowledges that Buyer has the right to engage a certified hygienist or industrial hygienist to test whether the Home 120 
or Lot has ever been used as a methamphetamine laboratory. Buyer has the Right to Terminate under § 24 .1, upon Seller’s receipt 121 
of Buyer’s written Notice to Terminate, notwithstanding any other provision of this Contract, based on Buyer ’s test results that 122 
indicate the Home or Property has been contaminated with methamphetamine, but has not been remediated to meet the standards 123 
established by rules of the State Board of Health promulgated pursuant to § 25-18.5-102, C.R.S. Buyer must promptly give written 124 
notice to Seller of the results of the test. 125
