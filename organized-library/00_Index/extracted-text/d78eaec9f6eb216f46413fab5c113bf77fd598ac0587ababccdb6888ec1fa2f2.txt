MW-191 (4/22)
This Schedule of Hours shall not apply to the employment of minors in: 
• Agriculture pursuits
• Domestic service in private homes
• Newspaper carriers
In accordance with New Jersey Child Labor Law N.J.S.A. 34:2-21-5, every employer that employs minors under 18 must 
keep and conspicuously post this Schedule of Hours with the following information recorded:
• Names of minors under 18 • Daily in & out times
• Schedule of hours* • Meal period in & out times
• Maximum daily and weekly hours permitted
Enforced by: Division of Wage and Hour Compliance • NJ Department of Labor and Workforce Development

This and other required employer posters are available free online at nj.gov/labor.
