3. Possession.  Seller may retain possession of the Property from date of Closing through ______________ (Possession Date) 30 
at _________ (Possession Time)(Term). However, Seller may cause an earlier termination upon 5 days written notice to Buyer. 31 
Such notice must be provided to the address, fax number or email address of the recipient as specified below or as otherwise 32

4. Maintenance.  If necessary, Buyer will, at Buyer’s sole expense, maintain, repair and replace the heating and cooling 37 
systems including ventilation and ducts, plumbing, electrical wiring, roof and structural components of the Property, all 38 
appliances in the Property owned by Buyer and the lawn sprinkler system, if any, if such items were functioning or working 39 
properly on the date of Closing. Unless the services are provided by a third party, e.g., owners’ association, Seller will maintain 40 
the Property as Seller previously maintained the Property to include, but not limited to, landscaping, snow removal and lawn 41 
care. Seller will provide timely notice to Buyer of any improvement requiring maintenance or repair. 42
