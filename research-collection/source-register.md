# U.S. State Source Register

**Snapshot date:** 2026-10-09
**Purpose:** route future collection and review of contracts, insurance materials, estate planning, real estate, employment, family, and court forms.
**Interpretation:** The table below is a routing plan, not proof that every state/source-family combination has been located. `source-identified` here refers only to the shared NAIC routing source. Court seed receipts and unresolved checks are in `snapshots/2026-10-09-02/states.json`; bar, legislature, and real-estate sources still need state-by-state identification.

## Source families

| Code | Source family | Primary use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `INS-NAIC` | NAIC Insurance Departments directory | Find the official insurance regulator and consumer/form routes for each state. |
| `COURT-STATE` | State judiciary and clerk/self-help portals | Official court forms, filing instructions, and procedural notices. |
| `BAR-STATE` | State bar or bar-approved form program | Lawyer-drafted or bar-approved practice forms where the program permits reuse. |
| `RE-STATE` | State real-estate commission or licensing regulator | Promulgated or regulator-published transaction forms. |
| `LEG-STATE` | State legislature/code publisher | Statutory disclosures, mandatory clauses, and execution rules. |
| `INSURER` | Insurer or filing repository | Policy forms and endorsements; permission and product-specific review required. |

## Canonical hubs and verified examples

| Area | Publisher | Canonical source | What it proves | What it does not prove |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Insurance routing | NAIC | https://content.naic.org/state-insurance-departments | Official state department directory and links to consumer/regulatory routes. | That a policy form is reusable, current, approved for a particular risk, or legally sufficient. |
| Real estate, Texas | Texas Real Estate Commission | https://www.trec.texas.gov/forms/one-four-family-residential-contract-resale | TREC identifies Form ID `20-19` and an effective date of July 1, 2026 on the form page. | That the form applies outside Texas or that it is a general residential-lease template. |
| Small claims, California | California Courts | https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/jcc-form/SC-100 | Official court self-help page links to the SC-100 PDF. | That the form is appropriate for another claim type or state. |
| Technology / business agreements | Common Paper | https://commonpaper.com/standards/ | Published standard-contract catalog. | State-specific enforceability or suitability for a particular deal. |
| Technology / business agreement license | Common Paper | https://commonpaper.com/standards/cloud-service-agreement/ | Cloud Service Agreement page identifies version 2.1, downloadable formats, and CC BY 4.0 links. | That every Common Paper document has the same version or that attribution/notice requirements have been satisfied in the product. |

## Collection queue

The next pass should collect one representative source per state and practice area before attempting state-wide template generation:

1. state insurance regulator directory entry and consumer/form route;
2. state court self-help or approved-form index;
3. state real-estate regulator form index;
4. state statutory requirements for wills, powers of attorney, residential leases, employment notices, and insurance disclosures;
5. state bar or licensed publisher source where a contract form is expressly reusable;
6. version, effective date, retrieval date, reuse terms, and hash for every retrieved artifact.

Insurer policy forms, proprietary commercial forms, and documents with unclear reuse terms remain `do-not-publish` until counsel and rights review are complete.
