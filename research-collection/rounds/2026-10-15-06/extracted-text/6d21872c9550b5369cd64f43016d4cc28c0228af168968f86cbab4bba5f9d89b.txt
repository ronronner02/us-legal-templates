NOTES: 
• Use this form to ask the court to modify (increase or decrease) a child support order but only if more than
thirty (30) days have passed since entry of the order establishing or modifying child support.
• This form can also be used as a counter-claim to either a motion to modify child support or a petition for
contempt for failure to pay child support. If you use this form as a counter-claim, you must also complete
and file a Certificate of Service.
• If you’re using this form to file an original motion, not a counter-claim, the clerk will then issue a summons.
The summons, your motion, and any attachments must be served on the opposing party. Learn more about
service at www.mdcourts.gov/ccservice. If the Child Support Administration is responsible for collecting child
support payments in your case, you must also send the local child support office copies of the summons, your
motion, and any attachments by first-class mail.
• Complete and attach a financial statement to this document. If parents’ combined gross monthly income (not
take home pay) is $30,000 or less, attach Financial Statement (Child Support Guidelines)
(CC-DR-030); if the combined gross monthly income is more than $30,000, attach Financial Statement
(General) (CC-DR-031).
