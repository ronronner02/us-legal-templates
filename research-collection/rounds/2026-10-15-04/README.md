## Scope and review limits

This round covers housing and eviction, civil procedure and property, foreign judgments, and small claims. Instructions are classified separately from filing forms. Foreign-state, Canadian, and other foreign-country judgment documents remain distinct. Blank requests do not constitute issued writs, orders, or judgments.

The four-page CV-067 application includes CV-191 Financial Affidavit, which is also supplied separately. Both original files are retained, with this content overlap recorded explicitly. SHA-256 distinctness counts original files and does not establish unique legal content.

Source catalog revision dates and printed revision markers are stored separately. Printed revisions are checked in extracted PDF text; HTTP Last-Modified is not treated as a publication date. No visual rendering, interactive-field testing, OCR, signature validation, legal adequacy review, or filing acceptance test was performed. PDFs with encryption/security flags were preserved unchanged and inspected through the parser standard password-free reader. Modification permissions, legal currency, reuse rights, and publication approval remain pending.

Delaware, New Jersey, North Dakota, Vermont, Washington, D.C., and Oregon returned access restrictions or stopped at robots verification. Their evidence is retained, and no documents were acquired from those sources this round. All inherited permission holds and access stops remain in effect. Maine clerk-purchase and attorney-issued summons/subpoena forms were excluded.

All acquired files must pass parser, identity, receipt, byte-count, source-link, and SHA-256 checks before final verification. Baseline tracked files are preserved byte for byte. Full-library semantic deduplication remains incomplete.
