## Review limits

These are blank official originals, not completed submissions. All acquired files must pass parser and expected-identity checks before this round is considered verified. No visual rendering, OCR, signature validation, legal adequacy review, or court acceptance test was performed. Printed revision candidates are preserved as leads and remain unverified unless specifically confirmed. HTTP Last-Modified is not a verified publication date.

Massachusetts includes a summons-and-complaint sample that the source identifies as not filing-ready, an eFiling-only supplementary process form, and separate court-specific agreements. West Virginia forms serve regulatory, licensing, complaint, or agency-disclosure purposes. They are not interchangeable transaction contracts.

Mass.gov terms reserve copyright and describe a fair-use exception; public availability does not establish republication permission. West Virginia reuse review also remains pending. Alaska form links are held for permission review after capturing its copyright restriction. These rights and publication decisions remain unresolved.

SHA-256 duplicate screening covers every baseline original. No claim of full-library semantic deduplication is made. Prior tracked files are preserved byte for byte. Only one bounded retry is allowed for a transport failure; no access-control workaround is used.
