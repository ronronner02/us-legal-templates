To transfer ​your salesperson/associate broker license, please follow these steps: ​The transferring licensee shall submit, in writing, to his/her current broker they are seeking a transfer from the current responsible broker. The​ current employing broker shall promptly sign and date the license with the last date of employment and email the signed license to wvrec@wv.gov and to the licensee to be uploaded with the Change of Employing Broker Form.
