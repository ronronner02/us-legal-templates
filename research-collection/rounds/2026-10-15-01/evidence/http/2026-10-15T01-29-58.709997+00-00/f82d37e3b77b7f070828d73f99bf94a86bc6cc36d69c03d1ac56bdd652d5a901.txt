Tuesday Topics Webinar Series for Employers Have questions about Maine's labor laws and workplace requirements? Join the Maine Department of Labor for Tuesday Topics, a monthly webinar series designed to help employers understand Maine's labor laws and workplace requirements. Each session covers a different topic and provides practical information and resources to help employers meet their responsibilities and support their employees. Tuesday Topics webinars are offered in two live Zoom sessions on the same day, at 10 a.m. and 1:30 p.m. Registration is not required; simply select a topic below to learn more about the session and join.
