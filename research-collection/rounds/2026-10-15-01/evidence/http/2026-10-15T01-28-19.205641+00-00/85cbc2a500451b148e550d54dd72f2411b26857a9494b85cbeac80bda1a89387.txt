Use the ready-to-use version (PDF), or use the editable version (Word) that you can add your own branding to. First contact First contact means when you have enough contact information about the person to be able to provide the pamphlet. First contact can include telephone, Internet, e-mail, in person, or similar methods. Delivery of pamphlet It can be given by email, Internet, regular mail, fax, hand, or similar method. Relationships and legal obligations The pamphlet describes the legal relationship between you and the consumer when you act as the consumer’s “agent.” It also discusses your legal obligations, called affirmative duties, to both buyers and sellers in a real estate transaction.
