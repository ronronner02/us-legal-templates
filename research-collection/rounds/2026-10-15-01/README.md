## Review limits

All files passed parser/container and expected-title checks. Visual PDF rendering, DOCX office rendering, OCR, signature validation, legal adequacy review, and product publication approval have not been performed. SHA-256 screening covered all 493 baseline originals; full-library semantic deduplication was not performed.

Maine construction source permits home-project copying/use of the model contract and change order. Several posters state they may be copied. These narrow statements do not establish broad modification, resale, or commercial redistribution permission. All reuse and legal reviews remain pending.

The 2027 wage poster is an upcoming edition as of October 15, 2026, with a stated rate effective January 1, 2027. It is retained alongside the 2026 edition. Printed revisions and rate-effective dates are cataloged separately; HTTP Last-Modified is not treated as a publication date.

The payroll form has garbled printed-revision extraction; no revision was inferred from its filename. The age-verification form carries a sensitive-information submission restriction. The PFML poster preserves historical scheduling wording without independent current-operation verification.
