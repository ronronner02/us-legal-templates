MAINE EQUAL PAY LAW 
Equal Pay Complaint Form 
Maine law requires that employees be paid the same wages as employees of the opposite sex for work that  
is of a comparable nature in skill, effort, and responsibility.    
If you are being paid less than an employee of the opposite sex for performing the same or a similar job and can  
answer any one of the following questions with a "yes," you can print and fill out the Equal Pay Complaint Form 
below. If you have any questions, please call 207-623-7900 or email mdol@maine.gov    
1. Does the other employee's job have comparable requirements relating to skill, effort, and responsibility?
2. Does the other employee have similar training, education or experience relating to the jobs performed?
3. Does your employer prohibit you from talking about your wages with your cowor kers?
