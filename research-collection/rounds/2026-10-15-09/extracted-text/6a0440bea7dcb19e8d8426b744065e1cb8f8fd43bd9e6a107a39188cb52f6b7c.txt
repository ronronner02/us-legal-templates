COMPLETING THE PLAINTIFF’S CLAIM AND NOTICE TO DEFENDANT (SMALL CLAIMS COURT) 
Use this form to file a Small Claims Case.  A Sma ll Claims Case is filed in the county court for an 
amount of $7,500.00 or less. Small claims cases can be filed in the county where the defendant lives 
or does  business, or in the county where the legal  dispute took place. 
You cannot file a case against a person who is in the military service of the United States.  
No one may file more than two claims in a calendar week or more than ten claims in a calendar year. 
An attorney cannot file the case for you, but you can ask an attorney for advice about your case. 
For more information about what you can or cannot do in this typ e of ca se, see this page: 
https://supremecourt.nebraska.gov/self-help/small-claims/filing-small-claims-case-nebraska
