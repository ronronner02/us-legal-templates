Use this form if you are the defendant in a Small Claims case and you would like 
to ask for a setoff or file a counterclaim in this case. You must give the reasons for 
your claim. 
Page 1 of 3 
If completed by an 
attorney, enter your 
Bar Number. 
Sign and date the 
form. Enter your 
printed name, your 
address, telephone 
number, and your 
email address. 
Enter the dollar 
amount of the 
counterclaim or 
setoff in the first 
box, or enter the 
value of the property 
you are asking to be 
returned in the 
second box. 
Enter the Reason 
for your 
counterclaim or 
setoff. 
Enter the name of 
the defendant. 
Enter the case 
number. 
Enter the name of 
the plaintiff. 
Choose the 
county using the
drop-down list.
