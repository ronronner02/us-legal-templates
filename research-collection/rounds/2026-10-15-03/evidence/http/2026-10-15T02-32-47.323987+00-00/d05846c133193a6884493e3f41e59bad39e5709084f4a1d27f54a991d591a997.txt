All filers, including attorneys, self-represented persons, and government agencies are required to file documents electronically in court. You may register to file electronically at https://www.iowacourts.state.ia.us/Efile/ . There is no fee to register. Use the latest version of Acrobat Reader to fill PDF forms. Get Acrobat free here .
