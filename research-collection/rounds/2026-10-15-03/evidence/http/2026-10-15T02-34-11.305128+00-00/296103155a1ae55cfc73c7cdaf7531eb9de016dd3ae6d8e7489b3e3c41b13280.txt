When you make an inquiry by e-mail, we do not post your inquiry on the web site. It is the policy of the Maryland Judiciary to preserve the privacy of personal records and to protect confidential or privileged information obtained through use of this web site. Such information will be disclosed publicly only in accordance with the Public Information Act or as necessary and permissible to carry out the duties of the Maryland Judiciary. This Privacy Policy does not cover personal or identifying information about litigants that may appear in published appellate court opinions posted on the Maryland Judiciary’s web site. This Policy addresses current Judiciary web site practices and does not cover future transactions such as e-commerce, e-filing, etc. If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please e-mail the [email protected] .
