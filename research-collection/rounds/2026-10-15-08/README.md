## Findings and review limits

This round adds 17 guardianship support originals, four name-change support forms, two alternate-service forms, one record-shielding waiver/release and one protective/peace-order brochure. It includes separately classified professional certificates, a fiduciary account, procedural guides and a conditional release. Blank templates do not establish appointments, assessments, consents, releases, notices, service, orders or completed reports.

All 25 printed dates agree with source-listed revision dates in extracted text. CC-GN-019 and CC-GN-022 print October 13, 2026. CC-DR-119 prints (01/2023) without a Rev. label. HTTP Last-Modified is not treated as publication evidence.

The source describes CC-GN-019 usage by examination/evaluation date: the new form must be used for examinations/evaluations on or after January 1, 2027, while the court may accept the new or specified prior versions for examinations/evaluations on or before December 31, 2026. This captured source statement is not independently verified law or a legal effective-date determination. The original professional categories are retained; CC-GN-022 states that only a licensed physician may complete it. No assessment was performed.

January 2021 instructions CC-GNIN-001 and CC-GNIN-002 are retained separately from previously collected October 2025/2026 petitions. The latter instructions reference older separate certificates. Compatibility with newer forms and the source-described 2027 transition requires review; no conclusion of invalidity is made.

New companions relate to earlier guardianship, name-change and shielding originals, but this is not a verified complete filing packet. MDJ-008, CC-DC-128, CC-DR-133, show-cause orders and other case-specific attachments were not acquired in this round. Parent/minor consent and objection, person/property roles, annual reports/accounting and motion/notice remain distinct.

Original bytes and network receipts are preserved. All inherited permission holds and access stops remain in force. Footer reserves copyright; download availability does not establish blanket reuse or publication rights. Visual rendering, interactive fields, calculations, signatures, legal currency, court acceptance, reuse and publication approval remain pending. No personal data was entered, no court or external interview submission occurred, and full-library semantic deduplication remains incomplete.
