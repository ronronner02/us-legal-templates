NOTICE TO PETITIONER 
Any individual who knowingly provides false information in a Petition for Peace Order is guilty of a misdemeanor and 
on conviction is subject to a fine not exceeding $1,000 or imprisonment not exceeding 90 days or both. 
If you are an employer, you must notify the employee before filing a Petition for Peace Order. An employer may not 
retaliate against an employee who does not provide information for or testify at a peace order proceeding.
