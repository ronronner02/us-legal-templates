circuit court forms Standard, statewide forms are required by all Wisconsin circuit courts for civil, criminal, family, guardianship, juvenile, mental commitment, probate and small claims cases. Information on mandatory use of court forms and frequently asked questions are available here . All statewide forms comply with the eFiling format requirements under Wis. Stat. § 801.18(3)(d) and contain the correct spacing and margins. The revision date on each form reflects substantive changes and is not used to reflect formatting changes.
